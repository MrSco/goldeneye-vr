# GoldenEye VR v0.5.9

Teammates are always at full volume on voice chat, now in Horde and co-op
too.

## Changes

- **Voice chat in Horde and co-op:** every player is a teammate, so you hear
  everyone at full volume wherever they are, whatever the host's **Voice
  mode**. Voices still come from the speaker's direction. Before, the host's
  Proximity setting made teammates quieter with distance (PR #206).
- **Voice mode in the launcher:** the setting only reaches free-for-all
  opponents, so it is greyed out in Horde, co-op and team battles, with a
  hint saying why. Team battles are unchanged: teammates at full volume,
  opponents quieter with distance.
- Game sound effects are unaffected.

## Testing and known issues

- Checked on Quest 3 with the pre-release build.
- Everything listed under v0.5.7's known issues still applies.

## Update and multiplayer compatibility

Download `GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.9.apk` or use **Update** in the headset launcher.
Updating over an earlier release keeps your settings. This APK contains no
GoldenEye, XBLA or Perfect Dark game data; use your existing game files.

Android version: `0.5.9`; versionCode: `89`; network protocol: `23`.
No network changes: v0.5.7, v0.5.8 and v0.5.9 play together; v0.5.6 and
older can't join. Each headset sets voice volume for itself, so players on
v0.5.7 or v0.5.8 still hear Horde and co-op teammates with the host's Voice
mode until they update.
