# GoldenEye VR v0.5.4

Horde mode in Archives with Call of Duty-style points, Mystery Box and Wall
Buys; an optional AI ally (Natalya); tracked heads and arms for other
players; Application SpaceWarp for Bean; 4x anti-aliasing; and Oddjob's hat.

**Multiplayer:** the network protocol goes to 22. Every player in a lobby
needs v0.5.4.

## Horde (PR #191, PR #193)

Pick **Horde** in the multiplayer launcher, solo or with up to four players.

- Endless waves of GoldenEye's armed guards in Archives, using their own AI.
  Waves grow, with 15-second breaks between them and a countdown on screen.
- Points: 500 to start, 10 per hit, 60 per kill, 100 per headshot kill. Your
  points show on the HUD and in the pause menu.
- **Mystery Box** (950 points): a random weapon from twelve. Grip it or press
  B to take it. After a few rolls the box can move to another spot (refunded).
- **Wall Buys**: six stations around the arena sell weapons and body armour;
  a weapon you already have refills its ammo at half price. Walk up and grip
  or press B.
- Co-op revives; players who join mid-wave spectate until the next break.
  Everyone down ends the run, with results and a retry from the host.
- Horde has its own friendly-fire setting; deathmatch settings are kept.

## AI ally: Natalya (#194, optional, off by default)

Turn on **AI ally (Natalya)** under **Play → Game rules…**, or for co-op and
Horde under the host's Match options. One Natalya joins the party in
GoldenEye's missions and in Horde.

- She follows the nearest living player, uses the game's own paths, doors and
  shooting, and engages enemies near you. Her shots never hurt players.
- Choose her weapon (PP7 by default, up to the RC-P90 and automatic shotgun),
  aggression (cautious, normal, aggressive), damage (25-400%), health
  (25-1000%) or make her invincible.
- In co-op and Horde the host runs her; she doesn't count toward waves, kills
  or revives, and everyone down still ends a Horde run.
- She appears beside the party, never on a player, and doesn't respawn after
  dying; the next mission or Horde retry brings her back.
- Missions with their own Natalya (Bunker II, Train, Jungle, Control) keep
  only her.

## Other players' heads and arms (PR #190)

Other players' heads turn with their headsets, and their arms and guns
follow their controllers (two-bone arm IK, with a torso that turns more
slowly). Players who lose tracking fade back to the old animation.

## Performance and image

- **SpaceWarp** (Screen tab, on by default while Bean is on): the game draws
  half the frames and the headset makes the ones between from motion and
  depth, so Bean holds 120 Hz while turning. A little more latency; text
  locked to your view can shimmer. Untick it to turn it off; tick it to keep
  it on without Bean. Guards that share a model each keep their own motion,
  so bodies on the ground don't smear in the frames the headset makes.
- **Anti-aliasing** (Screen tab: Off, 2x, 4x; 4x by default): smoother edges.
  4x is the most the Quest supports.
- Turning with Bean is a little smoother: draws that differ only in unused
  bounds are joined (PR #192).
- Launcher tooltips wrap instead of running off the right edge.

## Mods

- **Oddjob's hat** (PR #196): Mods → Get Oddjob's hat. Throwing knives look
  like the hat from 007 Nightfire in the Original, Bean and GoldenEye X hands,
  in flight and lying about. A small download, not in the APK.

## Update and multiplayer compatibility

Download `GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.4.apk` or use **Update** in the headset launcher.
Updating over an earlier release keeps your settings. This APK contains no
GoldenEye, XBLA or Perfect Dark game data; use your existing game files.

Android version: `0.5.4`; versionCode: `84`; network protocol: `22`.
Every player needs v0.5.4; v0.5.3 and older can't join.
