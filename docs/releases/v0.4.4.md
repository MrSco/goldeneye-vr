# v0.4.4

- Mission gadgets and items now appear in the hand in stereo, held like the
  mines: the Key Analyzer, Datathief, safe cracker, bomb defuser, door decoder,
  briefcases, keycards and keys, documents, floppies, tapes, money, gold bars,
  the bungee, the exploders and the other carried items with a model. The
  game's own data never marked them for a first-person model, so they showed an
  empty hand. Each has a fitted pose, scale and hand shape.
- **Gun fit** also fits the gadget in the right hand: the move stick moves it,
  the turn stick moves it up and down and (sideways) sizes it; with the right
  grip held the sticks turn it. Gadget fits save to `gevr_itempose.txt`.
- Gun fit stays on after **A** (save) and **B** (undo), so several guns and
  gadgets can be fitted in one go. In single player, **Menu + A** starts and
  ends Gun fit during play; it does nothing online or in split screen.
- **Send debug log** now includes `gevr_itempose.txt`, beside the gun fit in
  `goldeneye-vr.ini`, so player fits can become the defaults.
- The watch magnet (attract and repel), Geiger counter, identifier and
  communicator work like the laser watch and detonator: worn on the tracked
  watch arm, they act only once the right hand is brought to the watch and its
  pressing hand snaps on.
- Default fits from the headset for the grenade, Key Analyzer, Datathief and
  bolt key.

Android version name: `0.4.4`; version code: `57`; network protocol remains `17`.

## Validation

The Android NDK sources compile, the native v0.4.3, pause and all 59
multiplayer checks and 20 Android unit tests pass, and the signed release APK
builds and installs over v0.4.3 on a Quest 3.

## Headset validation

The maintainer fitted the gadgets in the headset with the new Gun fit, tested
the Menu + A shortcut and the watch-gadget grip, and approved the release.
Keep checking the less common mission items' fits on the levels that carry them.
