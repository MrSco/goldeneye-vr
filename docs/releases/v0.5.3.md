# GoldenEye VR v0.5.3

Optional ragdolls and limb dismemberment, and fixes for GoldenEye X's rocket
launcher in VR.

## Ragdolls and dismemberment (optional, off by default)

Turn them on under **Play → Game rules…**: **Ragdolls** and **Dismemberment**,
beside **Bodies stay**.

- Guards and players fall with physics in solo, co-op and multiplayer,
  including bots, with native, HD or Bean visuals. Each headset simulates its
  own bodies; they don't change health, hitboxes or anything sent online.
- The tuning is ported from Perfect Dark CE's ragdolls (Jolt physics): the
  human skeleton's masses and shapes, one-way knees and elbows, and how hard
  shots and explosions push.
- Bodies hit walls, floors, cliffs and slopes, and ride lifts and moving
  platforms. Doors push them aside.
- Shoot a body on the ground and it moves, bleeds where it's hit (native and
  Bean wounds), and can lose the part you hit.
- **Dismemberment:** strong enough shots, whole shotgun blasts on one limb,
  and explosions can take off arms, legs and heads. Two sliders set it:
  - **Damage needed** (0.5 to 10, default 1.0, so pistols and rifles can
    cut);
  - **Chance** (0 to 100%, default 40%).
- Cut ends get red caps that follow the edge of the hole, on native and Bean
  bodies.
- **Bodies stay** sets how many bodies remain (Off/fade, 12, 24 or 48) in
  every mode while ragdolls are on.

## GoldenEye X weapons

- The rocket launcher no longer shows the decals from the far side of its
  barrel through the near side.
- Loaded, it holds GoldenEye X's own rocket. GoldenEye's rocket, sized for
  its own launcher, poked its fins through the barrel.

## Other

- The zdebug diagnostics switch logs its draw counts again (PR 188).

## Validation

- Checked on Quest 3 on Dam with GoldenEye X weapons, Bean on and off:
  - ragdoll deaths, blasts and bodies thrown against walls and cliffs;
  - cuts with pistols, rifles, the shotgun and explosives, and their caps;
  - wounds on shot bodies;
  - the rocket launcher loaded, fired and reloaded.
- Native regressions run the real Jolt physics under ASan/UBSan:
  - deaths with 1 to 8 players and 12/24/48 bodies;
  - the skeleton, cut rules, blasts and caps;
  - walls, uncovered ground, doors and lifts.
- Not yet measured: Quest frame time with many bodies at once. Not yet played
  with two headsets.

## Update and multiplayer compatibility

Download `GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.3.apk` or use **Update** in the headset launcher.
Updating over an earlier release keeps your settings. This APK contains no
GoldenEye, XBLA or Perfect Dark game data; use your existing game files.

Android version: `0.5.3`; versionCode: `83`; network protocol: `21`.
v0.4.16 through v0.5.3 share the same multiplayer protocol.
