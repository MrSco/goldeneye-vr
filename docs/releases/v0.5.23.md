# GoldenEye VR v0.5.23

A loading screen, faster and smoother level starts, and a round of fixes from
an Apple TV player's report (#269, thanks!) and from flat players online.
Every level now loads behind a loading screen and starts already running
smoothly; with Bean on, a level's slow work is kept on disk, so it loads
faster the second time. **Network protocol 27, unchanged:** v0.5.23 plays
online with v0.5.22.

## Loading

- **A loading screen everywhere:** the GoldenEye VR logo and a gold bar,
  from START until the title and at every level, on every platform (in the
  headset on the virtual screen), Bean on or off. It fades in and out, and
  on a flat screen it stays up over a level's first, slowest frames, so the
  level appears already moving smoothly.
- **Bean loads before the intro:** Bean's level, sky and pictures are built
  while the level loads, instead of on its first frames. The picture used to
  freeze for seconds before the intro (most on an Apple TV HD) and the intro
  stuttered after.
- **Faster the second time:** Bean's slowest level work is kept on disk, as a
  graphics driver keeps its shaders, and used again next time. Dam with Bean:
  6.3 s to 3.7 s on an Apple TV HD, 3.4 s to 1.8 s on a Quest 3. The cache is
  rebuilt by itself when anything changes, and the system may clear it: then
  that level is just built again.
- **Shaders ready at boot:** the shaders a device has needed are compiled
  while the game starts, instead of the first time something new is drawn.
- **No stall the first time you open the GEVR menu in a mission** (the
  watch's arm loaded then).

## Settings and pads (#269)

- **Pad vibration** now starts at 30% (50% was too strong). Your setting is
  kept unless it was the old default.
- **Trigger feedback** has its own strength.
- **Look speed, Look deadzone and Invert look** for a pad's right stick, on
  every platform that takes a pad.
- **Head bob** can be turned off (Comfort), in the flat game too.
- **Frame rate:** Auto (the default), Full or Half. Auto drops a level to a
  steady 30 while the device can't keep up, and tries 60 again by itself; it
  no longer stays at 30 for good. At 30 the game now moves evenly (it felt
  like 15 before).
- **Reset all settings,** at the foot of the launcher's Play tab.
- **Saving settings no longer breaks up the sound** in the menus: each save
  stalled the game for about 130 ms on an Apple TV HD (folding the Friends
  panel, changing a setting).

## Apple TV

- **The 2nd generation Siri Remote** keeps its ring and centre click on
  tvOS 26, which had merged it into a remote without them. Tested with a
  1st generation remote; reports on a 2nd generation one welcome.

## Graphics

- **Bullet holes** lie whole on their walls on the PC, phones and Apple TV
  (they were cut to slivers, or missing with Bean).
- **Dam's grates** no longer flicker with Bean on.
- **Wall decals** on flat screens no longer fight their walls.

## Online

- **A flat player joining a match** can move, fire and use things with the
  keyboard and mouse again (Windows; a guest's keys were not read).
- **The mouse is no longer upside down** in matches: Look Upright is the
  real default, and a save folder left on Reverse by mistake is set right
  once.

## Help wanted and known issues

- **Untried on real hardware:** the 2nd generation Siri Remote on tvOS 26;
  co-op with two flat players online. Reports welcome.
- **Grenade smoke** turning with the headset (#249) is still open.
- Otherwise as listed for [v0.5.22](v0.5.22.md#help-wanted-and-known-issues).

## Update and compatibility

**Quest:** use **Update** in the headset launcher, or sideload
`GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.23.apk`. **PC:** the PC launcher's updater. On
iPhone/iPad, install the new IPA with SideStore, or use TestFlight
(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Vision Pro).

Android version: `0.5.23`; versionCode: `136`; network protocol: `27`.
v0.5.23 plays online with v0.5.22.

**Dedicated servers:** re-run your setup line to update.

- **Windows PC** (in PowerShell; sets up WSL for you if needed):
  `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.ps1 | iex`
- **Linux or a cloud server** (Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13, or newer):
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.sh | sudo bash`

No download contains game data: bring your own GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM, your
own patched ROM for a hack campaign, and for Bean, your own copy of the XBLA
release.
