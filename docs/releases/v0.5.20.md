# GoldenEye VR v0.5.20

Bean's guns reload by hand like GoldenEye X's: magazines come out, shells
and rounds go in one at a time. Bean's hands and arm are patched and sealed,
the scoped guns' lens stays on its eyepiece, and Bean's levels get five
fixes. Also an Apple TV build, on TestFlight and as a download. It plays
online with v0.5.15 to v0.5.19.

## Bean guns: reloading by hand

With **Manual Reloading** on and Bean's guns (the XBLA models), reloading
works as it does with GoldenEye X's guns. The release's models have no
magazines of their own, so each gun's magazine is cut out of its model.

- **Rifles and SMGs:** pull the magazine out of the gun, take a fresh one
  from your belt and push it into the well. A tinted placeholder shows where
  it goes.
- **Pistols:** drop the magazine with B/Y, then seat a fresh one. Their
  magazines are too small to pull out, in Bean and GoldenEye X alike.
- **Shotguns, the Cougar Magnum, the grenade launcher and the rocket
  launcher** load a round at a time: a shell, the Cougar's cylinder, a
  grenade or a rocket from your belt into the gun.
- **Sniper rifle:** reloads with GoldenEye X's gesture: lower it to your hip
  or across your chest.
- **No gun reloads itself** at an empty magazine while hand reloading is on.
- **Gun fit:** the magazine modes (held magazine, well) now fit Bean's guns
  too.
- The belt grab now goes by your hand, not your wrist.

## Bean hands and arms

- **The gun hand:** sculpted patches close the holes in the pistols' and
  knives' hands, and the hand is closed around the handle.
- **The off-hand arm:** sealed at the elbow, about 10% smaller, with the
  watch kept at its size and the wrist kept inside the watch band.

## Scopes and sights

- **Sniper rifle:** the scope's view follows the gun through a reload and
  stays square to the eyepiece.
- **KF7 and AR33:** their sight magnifier now shows whenever the gun is out,
  not only up close, and stays fixed to the gun.
- **The ammo count** sits beside a scope's lens instead of under it.
- **The weapon wheel** is no longer covered by the scope's view.
- **The red stereo crosshair** stays upright when you tilt your head.
- **Holding a gun with both hands,** the supporting hand now moves with the
  gun's recoil.

## Bean levels

- **Dam:** the gorge wall fades into the mountains again (#250).
- **Aztec:** the mainframe console's button is no longer see-through (#251),
  the flicker by the mainframe is gone (#252), and the floor logo's
  cut-out edges are clean (#253).
- **Smoke and flames** stay upright when you tilt your head (#249).

## Fixes

- **Ragdolls:** dead bodies no longer show through explosions and smoke.
- **Touch controls:** `TouchControls=0` in the ini turns the on-screen
  controls off, for mouse and keyboard on macOS through PlayCover (#254).

## Apple TV (experimental)

- **TestFlight:** the iPhone and iPad game, built for Apple TV. The
  unsigned `GoldenEyeVR-0.5.20-tvos.ipa` is below for sideloading with your
  own signing.
- **The ROM:** sent from a phone or computer on the same network to the
  address the TV shows.
- **Controls:** a game pad, or the Siri Remote as a pad. Tested in the
  simulator only; nobody has tried the remote or a pad on a real Apple TV yet.
- **iPhone and iPad TestFlight** is now the native app, as on Vision Pro,
  instead of the Quest APK in a compatibility layer.

## Help wanted and known issues

- **Bean reloads:** where the shotguns' shell goes in is a first guess.
  Report a round-at-a-time gun whose shell or round looks out of place.
- **Apple TV:** the Siri Remote and game pads on a real Apple TV.
- **Grenade smoke** turns with you when you turn with the stick, as it does
  on the flat screen. That is likely GoldenEye's own behaviour.
- Otherwise as listed for [v0.5.19](v0.5.19.md#help-wanted-and-known-issues).

## Update and compatibility

**Quest:** use **Update** in the headset launcher, or sideload
`GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.20.apk`. **PC:** the PC launcher's updater. On
iPhone/iPad, install the new IPA with SideStore, or use TestFlight.

Android version: `0.5.20`; versionCode: `133`; network protocol: `26`.
v0.5.15 to v0.5.20 play online together in GoldenEye's campaign; co-op in a
hack campaign needs v0.5.19 or v0.5.20 for everyone.

**Dedicated servers:** re-run your setup line to update.

- **Windows PC** (in PowerShell; sets up WSL for you if needed):
  `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.ps1 | iex`
- **Linux or a cloud server** (Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13, or newer):
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.sh | sudo bash`

No download contains game data: bring your own GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM, your
own patched ROM for a hack campaign, and for Bean, your own copy of the XBLA
release.
