# GoldenEye VR v0.5.19

A fifth ROM-hack campaign, GoldenEye with Mario Characters, and online co-op
in every hack campaign. Also co-op fixes for Control and Depot, the Dam
speedboat's island landing, a native Vision Pro app, and PC VR quitting
cleanly. It plays online with v0.5.15 to v0.5.18 in GoldenEye's own
campaign.

## Mario Characters

- **A new campaign:** n64squid's GoldenEye with Mario Characters V3.17, chosen
  beside START like Goldfinger 64, Tomorrow Never Dies and Mirror Mode. Patch
  your own GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM with `MarioCharactersV3.17.xdelta` and pick
  the result in the launcher, or copy it to the headset as `data/mario.z64`
  ([docs/rom-hacks.md](../rom-hacks.md)).
- Its own save file, its mission select over two pages, its guards and
  characters, music, objectives, Cheat Options (Play as Mario, Luigi, Peach
  or Daisy, Tiny Mode, Mushroom Mode, Enemy Rockets and more) and its pause
  watch, as on the cartridge.
- **Hands:** Mario's gloves get the same hand fixes as GoldenEye's own
  hands: no holes in the pistols' and knives' hands, the off hand, the
  watch arm's glove or the watch band.
- GoldenEye X, Bean and HD packs are off in this campaign, as in Goldfinger
  64 and Tomorrow Never Dies, and the START button now lists only what the
  chosen campaign loads.

## Co-op

- **Online co-op in every hack campaign:** Goldfinger 64, Tomorrow Never Dies,
  Mirror Mode and Mario Characters, from the Multiplayer tab, friends' joins
  and invites. Every player needs v0.5.19 and the same hack ROM. The lobby
  list names a game's campaign and switches yours to it when you have its
  ROM. In Mario Characters each player's Play as, Tiny Mode and Mushroom
  Mode are their own. Deathmatch stays GoldenEye's.
- **Control:** the door the mission shuts behind Natalya locked the rest of
  the party out once the first player was through. It now locks only once
  every standing player is through (#246).
- **Depot and any mission:** a teammate whose game never finished loading
  held the whole party: the exit stayed black and a downed player was never
  revived. A player still loading a minute after the host has loaded is
  dropped, and the mission goes on (#245).

## Fixes

- **Dam speedboat:** stepping off at the island put Bond on the guard hut's
  pad, with no floor under him. He now lands on the island's own floor (#247).
- **PC VR:** quitting from the SteamVR dashboard (or anything else that ends
  the VR session) now closes the game. It kept running until SteamVR killed
  it, so the next start offered a crash report (crash report fa49c715).

## Vision Pro

- The Vision Pro TestFlight app is now built natively from the game
  (an OpenXR runtime over visionOS's compositor) instead of running the
  Quest APK in a compatibility layer. It supports PlayStation VR2 Sense
  controllers. Testers: report anything that looks doubled, swims or stays
  black.

## Help wanted and known issues

- **Untested with two headsets:** the Control and Depot co-op fixes, and
  co-op in the hack campaigns (checked with two PC copies).
- **PC VR:** quitting from the SteamVR dashboard is untested on a headset.
- **Mario Characters:** dismemberment's cut caps are GoldenEye's neck size
  and shape, not the Mario characters'. GoldenEye's sleeve fixes don't
  carry to Mario's sleeves, which are new models.
- Otherwise as listed for [v0.5.18](v0.5.18.md#help-wanted-and-known-issues).

## Update and compatibility

**Quest:** use **Update** in the headset launcher, or sideload
`GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.19.apk`. **PC:** the PC launcher's updater. On
iPhone/iPad, install the new IPA with SideStore.

Android version: `0.5.19`; versionCode: `132`; network protocol: `26`.
v0.5.15 to v0.5.19 play online together in GoldenEye's campaign; co-op in a
hack campaign needs v0.5.19 for everyone.

**Dedicated servers:** re-run your setup line to update.

- **Windows PC** (in PowerShell; sets up WSL for you if needed):
  `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.ps1 | iex`
- **Linux or a cloud server** (Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13, or newer):
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.sh | sudo bash`

No download contains game data: bring your own GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM, your
own patched ROM for a hack campaign, and for Bean, your own copy of the XBLA
release.
