# GoldenEye VR v0.4.20

Project Bean visuals from your own copy of GoldenEye XBLA, the Goldfinger 64
and Tomorrow Never Dies campaigns, the Dam speedboat, and per-gun Gun fit you
can place by hand.

This APK contains no GoldenEye, XBLA or Perfect Dark game data. Use your
existing USA GoldenEye 007 ROM. Updating over an earlier release keeps your
settings.

## Project Bean (XBLA) visuals

- The launcher's **Mods** page imports the XBLA release's meshes, textures,
  fonts and pictures from your own copy of GoldenEye XBLA: its `.7z`, a
  `.zip`, or the Xbox 360 package. Press **Choose copy...** beside
  **XBLA / Bean** and pick it. Nothing is downloaded; the app keeps only the
  files Bean draws (about 360 MB). Select Bean under **Visuals** beside
  Original, HD and HD + AI.
- Characters, heads, guns, props, rooms, skies, the HUD, menus and the
  briefing folder use the release's art. Gameplay, collision and animation
  stay the original game's. This follows Dab's Mod's Bean renderer.
- First-person guns move their own slides, cylinders and drums, show the gold
  and silver guns' metal, and the rocket launcher carries its own round. The
  watch and weapon wheel show the Bean models.
- In the headset the off hand is Bean's own arm with the watch. Holding a gun
  with both hands, that arm holds it, pinned to the gun. The watch laser and
  detonator are pressed by Bean's hand.
- Toggle Bean during play in **watch > VR options > Mods and fun**, or hold
  one grip and click the right stick for a second.

## Gun fit

- Each gun keeps its own place in the hand and its own two-handed hold, so
  fitting one pistol no longer moves another. Bean's models start from fits
  made on Bean's arm.
- Hold the off hand's trigger in Gun fit to place by hand: holding a gun with
  both hands, the holding hand takes your off hand's place and turn on the
  gun; otherwise the gun moves with your off hand. The sticks still
  fine-tune, A saves and B undoes.

## Campaigns, Dam and multiplayer

- The launcher's campaign dropdown starts **Goldfinger 64 1.0** or **Tomorrow
  Never Dies 64 Expanded 06-22** from your own patched ROMs, with separate
  saves. Hack campaigns are solo only. See
  [Goldfinger 64 and Tomorrow Never Dies](../rom-hacks.md).
- Dam: board the speedboat at the north jetty and cross to the island
  (solo). Wall alarms take hits again.
- A tall character's view no longer rises through low doorways (Caverns'
  tunnel doors), in solo and multiplayer.
- The host's stage dropdown fits every stage's name.
- Multiplayer's temporary health and armour gauges appear when hit with
  radar enabled, whatever the comfort settings.

## Renderer

- With Bean, characters are skinned on the GPU and the world is batched by
  material, which lowers Dam frame times on Quest 3. Scopes and redrawn
  frames keep their own state.

## Validation and remaining checks

- 68 of the 69 Python regression files pass, including new native checks
  for the two-handed hold placed by hand, per-gun fits, doorway clearance on
  stairs and in multiplayer, and the stage dropdown's width; the campaign
  binder check needs a Goldfinger 64 ROM and was skipped. The Bean mapping
  audit passes all 456 native registrations. The signed ARM64 release builds
  with the existing release certificate.
- Bean, Gun fit, the doorway fix and the stage dropdown were played in the
  headset before release. Some Bean material effects (Dam and Complex water,
  Silo and Control floors) are still simplified, and the grenade is still
  held in the original hand: the release has no hand holding one.
- The campaigns, the speedboat and alarms need broader play; multiplayer
  checks on two headsets remain outstanding.

## Installation and multiplayer compatibility

Download `GoldenEye-VR-v0.4.20.apk` or use **Update** in the headset launcher.
Install it over your current version using your preferred installer.

Android version: `0.4.20`; versionCode: `76`; network protocol: `21` (unchanged).
v0.4.16 through v0.4.20 use the same multiplayer protocol.
