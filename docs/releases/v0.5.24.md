# GoldenEye VR v0.5.24

Two things for the headset: **teleport movement**, for players the stick
makes ill, and a **third step for reloading by hand**: racking the slide,
pulling the bolt or pumping the shotgun. Both are options, off unless you
turn them on. **Network protocol 27, unchanged:** v0.5.24 plays online with
v0.5.23 and v0.5.22.

## Teleport movement

**Comfort → Movement: Teleport** (launcher or the in-game settings; off by
default). In the headset, on foot.

- **Aim and jump:** push the move stick forward and an arc leaves that hand,
  with a ring where it meets the floor. Let go and the view blinks to black
  for a moment: you are standing on the ring. Nothing slides.
- **Hops:** flick the stick back, left or right for a one metre hop.
- **Cancel:** point at your feet, or anywhere the mark is red, and let go.
- **It is not a shortcut,** in a mission or online:
  - a jump goes only where you could have walked in a straight line. It is
    tested with the walk's own collision check, so walls, shut or half-open
    doors, glass, crates, guards, ledges and crouch-only gaps all stop it,
    and the ring stops in front of them;
  - a jump costs its length from a stamina that holds 6 m and refills a
    little slower than running (the white band round the ring), so over any
    stretch a teleporting player covers less ground than a running one;
  - a quarter second between jumps, and no moment when you can't be hit.
- **Online** other players see a jump as a quick dash. Nothing new is sent, so
  it works against older builds.
- While a grip aims, the stick no longer leans or ducks you in this mode (that
  is a slide of the view too): duck with your body, or with stick-click
  crouch.

More in [teleport](../teleport.md).

## Reloading by hand: rack it

Part of **Hand reload WIP** (Controls → Gestures), with GoldenEye X's guns or
Bean's. A gun you fire dry now needs a third step after its fresh magazine.

- **Pistols:** `RACK THE SLIDE`. Grip the rear of the slide with your free
  hand, pull back, let go.
- **Submachine guns, the KF7, AR33, RC-P90 and sniper rifle:** `PULL THE
  BOLT`, the same at the bolt handle.
- **The shotgun:** `PUMP IT` after every shot. Keep hold of the fore-end and
  slide that hand back and forward.
- **The automatic shotgun** cycles itself; pump it once after it runs dry.
- **Change a magazine before the gun runs dry** and there is nothing to rack:
  a round is still chambered.
- **A ring on the gun** shows where to take hold. **Gun fit**'s reload page
  moves it: put your free hand where you want it and squeeze its grip.
- **Two-handed guns let go while they wait:** the two-handed hold releases the
  gun until it is racked, so your front hand slides along it instead of
  turning it, and takes it again by itself afterwards.
- **Skipping it:** touching the gun to your belt loads and chambers it, as it
  always has, and dual wielding reloads that way with no rack.
- The count on your hand reads 0 until the gun is racked. The magazine's
  rounds are not lost: they are held back, and return to your reserve if you
  drop it again.

Also for hand reload:

- **Bean's sniper rifle has a magazine.** Its model had none, so the game
  makes one: a small box under the receiver, taken out and put in like the
  other rifles'. It is drawn only with Hand reload on.
- **Bean's shotguns take their shells from the gun,** from the shells they
  carry (the ring), not from your belt, so a hip holster is never in the way.
- **No pistol in the way of a magazine:** with a gun's magazine out, your free
  hand finds no holster at the hips or belt, only the magazine.

More in [racking](../hand-reload-rack.md).

## Help wanted and known issues

- **Teleport, untried:** two headsets in one match; every stair in every
  level (a stair the ring will not cross is a bug: tell us the level and the
  place); the blink at 120 Hz and with SpaceWarp.
- **Rack, untried:** GoldenEye X's guns (the release was played with Bean's);
  left-handed mode. The places you take hold of are fitted for the KF7 and
  AR33 and guessed for the rest: Gun fit moves them.
- **Not yet:** a host rule to forbid teleporting in a match; a bolt or handle
  that moves on the submachine guns and rifles (only the pistols' slides
  move); the sniper rifle's two-handed bat with Bean's models.
- Otherwise as listed for [v0.5.23](v0.5.23.md#help-wanted-and-known-issues).

## Update and compatibility

**Quest:** use **Update** in the headset launcher, or sideload
`GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.24.apk`. **PC:** the PC launcher's updater. On
iPhone/iPad, install the new IPA with SideStore, or use TestFlight
(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Vision Pro).

Android version: `0.5.24`; versionCode: `137`; network protocol: `27`.
v0.5.24 plays online with v0.5.23 and v0.5.22.

**Dedicated servers:** re-run your setup line to update.

- **Windows PC** (in PowerShell; sets up WSL for you if needed):
  `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.ps1 | iex`
- **Linux or a cloud server** (Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13, or newer):
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.sh | sudo bash`

No download contains game data: bring your own GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM, your
own patched ROM for a hack campaign, and for Bean, your own copy of the XBLA
release.
