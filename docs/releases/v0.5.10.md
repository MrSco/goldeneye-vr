# GoldenEye VR v0.5.10

Facility's flicker with Project Bean is fixed, glass no longer doubles with
SpaceWarp, and two crash reports are dealt with.

## Changes

- **Facility flicker with Bean:** the one-frame fog-coloured or black wedges
  over the floor were guards' detailed guns drawn with a broken placement,
  their triangles spread thousands of units around your head. Facility has
  more guards than the game kept slots for, so a guard could lose its slot
  between the two halves of its own draw. Only guards whose gun is actually
  drawn take a slot now, and a slot handed on is finished first. In 10
  minutes of Facility on Quest 3: no broken frames (about 18 before), frame
  rate unchanged. Guards' guns should also stop dropping out for a frame
  (PR #209).
- **SpaceWarp through glass:** with SpaceWarp on, what you saw through
  Facility's windows doubled and blurred as your head moved. Glass no longer
  hands its own motion to what is behind it (PR #209).
- **Silo crash (report 3706d27a):** with many bodies lying around, Silo's
  frame could outgrow its display-list buffer and stop the game. The buffer
  is now twice as large (PR #208).
- **Valve Lepton "ended unexpectedly" (report 79a638bb):** a restart by the
  Android container is no longer reported as a crash (PR #208).

## Testing and known issues

- The Facility and glass fixes were checked on Quest 3 with the pre-release
  build, with SpaceWarp on and off.
- The Silo fix was checked against the crash report's numbers, not by
  playing Silo with 24 bodies.
- Everything listed under v0.5.7's known issues still applies, except the
  Facility edge wedges, which were this flicker.

## Update and multiplayer compatibility

Download `GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.10.apk` or use **Update** in the headset launcher.
Updating over an earlier release keeps your settings. This APK contains no
GoldenEye, XBLA or Perfect Dark game data; use your existing game files.

Android version: `0.5.10`; versionCode: `90`; network protocol: `23`.
No network changes: v0.5.7 to v0.5.10 play together; v0.5.6 and older can't
join.
