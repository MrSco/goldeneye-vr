# GoldenEye VR v0.5.26

**Hand reload is now on by default** with every model set, and multiplayer
shows who reloads by hand, with an optional filter. The launcher gets a
**Graphics** tab with per-headset defaults measured on a Quest 3. Doors and
Dam's truck now stop for every guard, body and Bond, ragdolls or not, and
the lobby gets **Add friend**. **Network
protocol 27, unchanged:** v0.5.26 plays online with v0.5.25, v0.5.24,
v0.5.23 and v0.5.22.

## Hand reload, gestures and guns

- **Hand reload is on by default** (Controls → Gestures, no longer "WIP"). It
  works with GoldenEye's own guns, GoldenEye X's and Bean's. It is switched on
  once for existing players too, who had it off only by the old default.
  After that it's your choice.
- **Grip use goes with hand reload** on every model set: B/Y eject magazines,
  so doors and switches take the grip. Before, this applied only with
  GoldenEye X. Your own Grip use choice comes back when hand reload is off.
- **Force pull is one setting.** It always takes a guard's gun and always
  shows the glow and line. Pull disarm, Pull visuals and Auto pickup are gone,
  and walking over a gun always picks it up.
- **Gun fit with the gun in the off hand:** the magazine and reload places can
  be fitted when the primary hand is empty (original guns, GoldenEye X, Bean).
  Unarmed, primary-hand magazine and bat fits are saved.
- **Two-handed rifle bat** (all model sets): it swings like a baseball bat,
  from the gun wrist with the hands together and along the line between them
  when they're apart. Y in Gun fit switches between the two. The leftover PP7
  barrel is gone from the original sniper bat.

## Doors, bodies and Dam's truck

- **Every guard, body and Bond stops doors and Dam's truck.** On the Quest,
  Android, Linux and Apple builds, the check read part of a memory address
  instead of a guard's "hidden" flag. So whether a guard or body held a door,
  or the truck stopped for Bond in third person, came down to where it sat in
  memory. Windows happened to read the right flag.
- **Doors and the truck behave the same with Bodies stay and ragdolls on or
  off**, so runs compare and online matches stay in sync. A dead guard holds
  a door, or the truck, for the original game's fade time, then fades. A body
  kept longer fades as soon as a door or the truck touches it, and ragdolls
  they touch fade. A falling guard still blocks a door. (Door fix by
  @Kelly-D-Ripper, #290, reworked.)
- **The ghost-race ghost** never stops a door or the truck.
- **Third person:** backed up against the truck or another solid object, the
  camera comes in to Bond's eyes, as it does at a wall.

## Graphics

- **New Graphics tab** in the launcher (Comfort & display → Graphics) and in
  game, with the same options in both places:
  - refresh rate, SpaceWarp, Resolution, anti-aliasing (headset and flat
    game) and anisotropic filtering;
  - texture filtering (Pixelated, Smooth or N64 accurate);
  - frame rate, fullscreen and screen shape.
- **Per-headset "Auto" defaults**, measured on a Quest 3 at every refresh rate:
  - Quest 3/3S: 90 Hz, 1.25x Resolution, 16x anisotropic.
  - Quest 2/Pro: 72 Hz, 1x, 4x anisotropic (estimated).
  - PC: 16x anisotropic.
- **Limits by device:**
  - Resolution goes up to 1.5x on Quest 3/3S, 1.25x on other standalone
    headsets and 2x on PC VR.
  - Anti-aliasing goes up to 4x on Quest and 8x on PC VR.
  - Settings likely to drop frames warn in their rows.

## Multiplayer, friends and ghosts

- **Reload mode:** every game is listed by default, and you choose whether to
  filter.
  - The lobby roster has a Reload column (Hand, Button, or "—" for bots and
    older builds).
  - A host can tick **Hand reload only**, beside "VR players only". A joiner
    who reloads with the button is told how to turn hand reload on.
  - The Join list has **Hide games with button reload**, off by default.
  - It works online, on LAN and on dedicated servers.
- **Add friend from the lobby:** click a player for their friend code, Add
  friend, and Kick for the host. The roster's names have room again.
- **Add friend on the ghost boards**, from the same friend code.
- **Ghost runs:**
  - Teleport movement is unranked, and the launcher warns before a run that
    won't count.
  - Each run notes how you reloaded (by hand, with the button, or both). The
    boards and the ghost page show it, and it never affects the ranking.
- **Join list (#281):** a Servers filter (All games, Dedicated servers,
  Player-hosted games, or a custom server by name or IP, with `:port`), and
  recently joined servers are remembered. Community servers can ask to be
  listed with the "Dedicated server listing" issue form.
- **Proximity voice:** opponents are at full volume within 8 m and silent past
  25 m. Before, they were at full volume to 30 m and never below 10%, so you
  could hear them across the map. Teammates, Horde, co-op and spectators are
  unchanged.

## Flat screens, PC and the launcher

- **Crouch key** for flat play: C or Left Ctrl toggles crouch. It can be
  rebound on the keyboard and gamepad pages.
- **Speedrun restart:** with the speedrun timer, ghost race or LiveSplit on,
  Backspace on PC (rebindable) or both grips + holding the left stick click for
  a second in VR restarts a solo mission from its intro, with the run's timing
  reset.
- **Flat online pause:** the Enter that opens it no longer presses Resume at
  once. The Match tab says map, weapon and scenario changes apply next round.
- **iCloud sync on the TestFlight builds** for iPhone, iPad and Vision Pro:
  settings and saves sync between your devices, ROMs go to your private iCloud,
  and texture packs too with "iCloud: big packs". A device joining for the
  first time takes the cloud's settings. Apple TV follows in a later build, and
  the sideloaded IPAs can't sign with iCloud, so they don't sync.
- **Launcher cleanup:** HD textures moved to Mods/Fun, Reset sound is gone (the
  #229 sound bug it worked around is fixed), and the "This run won't count"
  buttons fit their labels.
- **Back to launcher** no longer leaves the game hung on Quest: the game
  restarts only once the old process has gone.

## Update and compatibility

**Quest:** use **Update** in the headset launcher, or sideload
`GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.26.apk`. **PC:** the PC launcher's updater. On
iPhone/iPad, install the new IPA with SideStore, or use TestFlight
(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Vision Pro).

Android version: `0.5.26`; versionCode: `139`; network protocol: `27`.
v0.5.26 plays online with v0.5.25, v0.5.24, v0.5.23 and v0.5.22. An older
build doesn't show reload modes, and older hosts don't check "Hand reload
only".

**Dedicated servers:** re-run your setup line to update.

- **Windows PC** (in PowerShell; sets up WSL for you if needed):
  `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.ps1 | iex`
- **Linux or a cloud server** (Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13, or newer):
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.sh | sudo bash`

No download contains game data: bring your own GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM, your
own patched ROM for a hack campaign, and for Bean, your own copy of the XBLA
release.
