# GoldenEye VR v0.5.18

Faster online matchmaking, clearer voice chat, GoldenEye X hand-reload
improvements, co-op Frigate fixes, and fixes for Bean, flat play, iOS and
Windows. It plays online with v0.5.15, v0.5.16 and v0.5.17.

## Online

- **Joining is faster.** The lobby service now pushes join requests and
  answers to the host and joiner over a live connection. A join reaches the
  host in under half a second instead of on its next check, up to 4 seconds
  later. A waiting host also stops checking in over and over. Where the
  connection isn't available, the game checks in as before.
- **Dedicated servers** get the same faster joins through the lobby bridge.

## Voice chat

- **Fewer dropouts:** each voice packet now carries a backup of the one
  before it, so a single lost packet is rebuilt instead of guessed. In a
  test with the game's settings, a lost moment came back far closer to the
  original.
- **Steadier on bad connections:** a voice that keeps running dry now
  buffers a little more, up to 120 ms, and goes back down once the
  connection settles.
- **Quest: less echo.** The mic now uses the headset's voice-call mode, which
  turns on its echo canceller, so other players should hear less of your
  game sound and of their own voices coming back.

## GoldenEye X guns (hand reload)

- **A label over the gun** says where its magazine is and what to do next:
  EMPTY: EJECT B/Y, MAG OUT: GRAB ONE, INSERT MAGAZINE, MAG IN, and NO AMMO.
  It shows only while there's something to do.
- **The PP7s' and DD44's slide** moves on every shot, stays locked back on
  the last round, and closes when a loaded magazine goes in.
- **The PP7s' and DD44's magazine** sits a little lower in the grip, so its
  base shows below it and an empty grip is easy to spot.
- **Shotgun shells, rockets, grenade rounds and the Cougar's speedloader**
  now show at your belt, like magazines do, and light up when your hand can
  take one. While one is there, the holstered pistol on that hip steps
  aside so you grab the round, not the pistol.
- **The Cougar's and the Golden Gun's grips** are no longer hollow
  underneath.

## Co-op

- **Frigate's bomb defuser** works for every player. A teammate's defuse
  could fail, and blow up the bomb, when another player was nearer the
  bomb's script (#241).
- **Hostage casualties** count for the whole team. On 00 Agent, Frigate
  fails "Rescue hostages" once more than one hostage dies, as in the
  original game; the "Unacceptable hostage casualties" message now shows
  whichever player shot them.

## Graphics

- **Bean:** black, unlit faces are lit or left out, such as the slab by
  Aztec's shuttle bay.
- **Bean, 16:9 screens:** the legal screen's Rare logo is no longer cut off
  on the right. Bean's models on 16:9 menu screens were drawn too wide.
- **Flat play (PC, phone, Quest 2D screen):** the hands and the watch arm
  are solid again; they looked hollow as the watch came up (#238).

## PC, iPhone and iPad

- **Windows:** the game no longer opens a console window beside it.
- **iPhone/iPad:** swiping the game away no longer brings up a crash report
  on the next launch, and a report's Send button can't send it twice.
- **iPhone/iPad:** game controllers are declared to iOS, and the D-pad
  moves GoldenEye's menus.

## Help wanted and known issues

- **Untested in a real match:** the voice changes (echo, lost packets,
  buffering) and the co-op defuser fix. Reports from a two-player round
  help.
- Otherwise as listed for [v0.5.16](v0.5.16.md#help-wanted) and
  [v0.5.15](v0.5.15.md#known-issues).

## Update and compatibility

**Quest:** use **Update** in the headset launcher, or sideload
`GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.18.apk`. **PC:** the PC launcher's updater. On
iPhone/iPad, install the new IPA with SideStore.

Android version: `0.5.18`; versionCode: `131`; network protocol: `26`.
v0.5.15, v0.5.16, v0.5.17 and v0.5.18 play online together.

**Dedicated servers:** re-run your setup line to update (faster joins
through the lobby bridge).

- **Windows PC** (in PowerShell; sets up WSL for you if needed):
  `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.ps1 | iex`
- **Linux or a cloud server** (Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13, or newer):
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.sh | sudo bash`

No download contains game data: bring your own GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM, and
for Bean, your own copy of the XBLA release.
