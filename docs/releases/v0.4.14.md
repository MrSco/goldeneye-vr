# GoldenEye VR v0.4.14

Bots that handle doors, soft collision between players, a new hit immunity
rule, and a fix for getting stuck inside objects.

> **Bring your own game.** This APK contains no GoldenEye or Perfect Dark game
> data. You need a legally obtained ROM of the USA GoldenEye 007 cartridge you
> own. Installing over an earlier release keeps your ROM and settings.

> **Multiplayer compatibility.** The network protocol is now **20**. Players on
> v0.4.13 or older can't join a v0.4.14 game, or the other way round: everyone
> in a match needs v0.4.14.

## Bots and doors — PR #153

Bots were getting stuck at doors, most of all on Facility. From five
playtest rounds with seven bots:

- **Doors on the way.** Bots open doors the way GoldenEye's own guards do: the
  door is swung away from the bot and opened, without pressing B. B toggles a
  door, so a bot pressing it repeatedly used to shut doors it had just opened.
- **Only doors that need it.** A bot opens only a shut or closing door, and
  only doors a player could open (no key, no padlock). Other players see the
  door move.
- **The door it's pressed against.** A bot standing against a door now
  notices it:
  - if the door is shut or closing, the bot opens it;
  - if the door is opening but stalled against the bot, the bot steps back
    out of its swing.
- **A leaf in the way.** A two-way door whose open leaf blocks the bot's path
  is shut and reopened the other way, but never from inside the doorway.
- **Getting stuck.** Climbing counts as progress, and the first seconds after
  spawning don't count as being stuck.

## Soft collision between players — PR #153

- **No more pinning.** Online, players no longer block one another, so an
  enemy standing in front can't pin you in a corner and a corridor can't trap
  the party.
- **Overlapping players are pushed apart and slowed.** The push is gentle and
  grows with the overlap, and you move 30% slower while you overlap.
- **Walls still hold.** The push goes through the normal collision, so it
  can't move you through a wall or a guard.

## Hit immunity rule — PR #153

GoldenEye ignores a hit for half a second to a second after the last one.
Fast guns felt as if most of their shots missed. It's now a host match rule:

- **Short** (the default): a hit is ignored for a quarter of a second after the
  last. A fast gun lands about four hits a second.
- **None:** every hit counts, as in Perfect Dark.
- **GoldenEye:** the original rule.

It's a **Hit immunity:** drop-down in the launcher's match rules and **HIT
IMMUNITY** on the pause menu's **Rules** tab. Solo and co-op keep GoldenEye's
rule.

## Other fixes — PR #153

- **Stuck inside objects.** A player who lands inside an object can walk out.
  On Facility, dropping from the start vent onto the toilet used to trap you
  for good.
- **On-screen messages.** Online, getting hit no longer hides the top message
  or the match clock. "MATCH STARTS IN" was blinking while bots shot you
  during warmup.

## Known issues

- **Climbing on Facility.** Bots can still pause near the top of the climb at
  Facility's upper floor.
- **Soft collision on two headsets.** It has been tested with bots on one
  headset, not yet with two people.

## Validation

- **Headset.** Five rounds on Facility with the user hosting seven bots, each
  watched live through the game log:
  - stuck spells fell from dozens at single doors to a few a minute, scattered;
  - the Short rule counted about half the hits on the player, as designed;
  - no crashes.
- **Tests.** The native regression suites pass, including 71 multiplayer
  tests and the new soft-collision test.
- **Build.** The release APK builds and is signed with the existing release key.

## Installation

Use **Update** in the headset launcher when v0.4.14 appears, or install
`GoldenEye-VR-v0.4.14.apk` over your current version with SideQuest or ADB. It
uses the existing release signing key. See the
[install guide](https://github.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr#-install-it-in-5-steps).

Android version: `0.4.14`; versionCode: `69`; network protocol: `20` (v0.4.13
and older can't join v0.4.14 games).
