# GoldenEye VR v0.5.7

Horde grows up: four more maps, special rounds, tougher enemies and two-gun
rules, from players' feedback. Wreck's Mirror Mode arrives as a campaign, with
Bean and HD packs flipped to match. Facility's green flashes are gone.

## Horde

- **Maps:** Archives, **Bunker I**, **Bunker II**, **Control** and
  **Facility**. The host picks the map and a **starting wave** (1-50) in the
  Horde rules column; a later start brings the points the skipped waves
  would have paid.
- **Special rounds:** waves 5, 15, 25... are an **Oddjob swarm** (many, small,
  fast, weak); waves 10, 20... are **boss rounds** (Jaws, Xenia, Oddjob,
  Trevelyan and Ourumov, one to three by party size).
- **Enemies:** armoured Janus marines from wave 4 and Janus special forces
  from wave 8, both a gun up. Guards run faster and shoot straighter as the
  waves climb.
- **Two guns:** a third from a wall buy or the box replaces the one in your
  hand (or the older one); the prompt says which. The Moonraker Laser has a
  finite pool.
- **Doors:** every door the level lets open is open and stays open; wall buys
  and the box stand clear of doorways.
- **Ammo crates:** one death in three drops one (every boss does), each worth
  twice as much, and you can pick them up at full ammo. Buying a gun adds its
  magazine to the rounds you hold.
- **Bodies:** a Horde run keeps at most 12 ragdolls, whatever Bodies stay is
  set to; 22 of them slowed the game after three waves.
- From PR #201's two-headset playtest: shared enemy ammo drops, the whole
  starting party plays wave one, damage to a joining player, remote arm
  poses, corpse cost, guards stalling in doorways.

## Mirror Mode (Wreck)

- Wreck's August 2017 **Mirror Mode** is a solo campaign beside GoldenEye,
  Goldfinger 64 and Tomorrow Never Dies: patch your own GoldenEye (USA) ROM,
  choose **Add Mirror Mode ROM...** in the campaign dropdown beside START,
  then **Mirror Mode (Wreck)**. It keeps its own save; the watch runs
  backwards.
- **Bean and HD packs work in it.** Bean's rooms, sky and the objects Wreck
  flipped are flipped the same way, the legal page's pictures turn over in
  place, and the stage pictures are turned over. HD packs replace everything
  Wreck left alone. Online play and GoldenEye X are off for it.
  [Mirror Mode](https://github.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/blob/main/docs/mirror-mode.md).

## Fixes

- **Facility's green flashes:** with anti-aliasing on, an eye image drawn
  straight in could reach the headset holding only the fog colour. Every
  multisampled frame now goes through the one-pass replay. The world behind
  the pause menu, which flickered the same way, does too.
- **Posters and signs skewing with SpaceWarp:** a decal no longer carries its
  own motion; it moves with the wall it is painted on.
- **Spawns in every mode** land on the floor and clear of other players and
  objects. A co-op player joining a mission under way starts beside a
  teammate, or by a pad in that teammate's room when there is no room beside
  them (a joiner on Facility had started outside the level).
- The Bean Runway plane keeps its hull at a distance; coincident Bean room
  layers on the same image no longer fight.

## Testing and known issues

- Checked on Quest 3: Horde solo on Archives (the pause menu, the second round),
  Facility without flashes, Archives posters with SpaceWarp, a Mirror Mode
  mission, Mirror Mode with Bean (rooms, objects, legal screen).
- Not yet played: Bunker, Control and Facility Horde, swarm and boss rounds
  on the headset, two-headset Horde with this build, a co-op join on Facility.
- Runway's mounted guns that still shoot after a timed mine: not reproduced.
  This build logs every hit on them; a log from the moment it happens would
  settle it.
- Facility: a few fog-coloured wedges at room edges remain (seen with
  anti-aliasing off too).

## Update and multiplayer compatibility

Download `GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.7.apk` or use **Update** in the headset launcher.
Updating over an earlier release keeps your settings. This APK contains no
GoldenEye, XBLA or Perfect Dark game data; use your existing game files.

Android version: `0.5.7`; versionCode: `87`; network protocol: `23`.
Everyone in a lobby needs v0.5.7: v0.5.6 and older can't join.
