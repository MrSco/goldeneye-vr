# GoldenEye VR v0.5.11

XBLA / Bean gains the GoldenEye XBLA Community Edition from your own updater,
and a batch of Bean fixes found while testing it: Jungle's sky band, Runway's
plane and gun, Bond's head in Frigate's intro, and the water and clouds of
GoldenEye's own sky.

## New

- **XBLA Community Edition:** on the Mods page, **CE...** on the XBLA / Bean
  row applies the community's updater (`CommunityEditionUpdaterV6.zip`, which
  you supply) to your imported copy: new skydomes, Frigate's water, props,
  doors, icons and text, with the Community Edition's fog. A **CE** box in the
  same place switches it from the next level. Nothing of it is shipped or
  downloaded; the updater applies only to an unmodified GoldenEye XBLA
  release. A copy imported before this version must be imported again first,
  since the import now keeps the files the update patches (PR #210,
  [docs](https://github.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/blob/main/docs/bean-community-edition.md)).
- **SpaceWarp on the watch:** VR: Display has a SpaceWarp row, which takes
  effect at once.

## Fixes

- **Jungle with Bean:** a band of wild coloured triangles over the canopy in
  the headset. The eyes are redrawn from a game frame's draws until the next
  is ready; Jungle's foliage outgrew that buffer's 4 MB per frame, so a redraw
  read vertices the next frame had already written. It is 16 MB now.
- **Runway with Bean:** the plane and the gun turret showed only their
  propeller and barrel. Their bodies were bound to a part of the model that
  draws nothing.
- **Frigate's intro with Bean:** Bond had no head. His suit head has a near and
  a far version; Bean's head was on the far one only.
- **GoldenEye's sky and water:** Frigate's water (Original look) had a band of
  coloured speckles that changed from run to run. A picture loaded with its
  mip levels was uploaded at the size of everything loaded with it, not of the
  picture.

## Testing and known issues

- Checked on Quest 3: the Community Edition applied from the user's updater
  with CE content in game; Jungle, Runway and Frigate's intro with Bean;
  Frigate's water in the Original look; the watch's SpaceWarp row.
- Everything listed under v0.5.7's known issues still applies.

## Update and multiplayer compatibility

Download `GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.11.apk` or use **Update** in the headset launcher.
Updating over an earlier release keeps your settings. This APK contains no
GoldenEye, XBLA or Perfect Dark game data; use your existing game files.

Android version: `0.5.11`; versionCode: `91`; network protocol: `23`.
No network changes: v0.5.7 to v0.5.11 play together; v0.5.6 and older can't
join.
