# GoldenEye VR v0.5.21

Bean's look (the XBLA release) gets the last of the fixes from Dab's Mod:
levels drawn out to where their fog is whole, props and guards shot where
they are drawn, the release's monitors, hats, welded seams and dozens of
smaller mends. The labels on the guns in your hand no longer flicker,
multiplayer shows which device everyone plays on, and ghost races keep
their drops. Plus the Apple TV fixes. **Network protocol 27:** v0.5.21
plays online only with v0.5.21.

## Bean levels

- **Draw distance:** rooms and props are drawn until the fog is whole, so
  Surface, Runway and the Dam no longer pop in at the old limit. Surface's
  frame rate on the Quest is unchanged.
- **The far backdrop** fades into the fog by its distance from you.
- **Level fixes from the Community Edition, on every look:** Bunker II's
  stairs, and the Silo, Control, Surface and Egyptian setups. These change
  what everyone loads, hence protocol 27.
- **Frigate:** the mirrored hull no longer shows through the sea. With the
  Community Edition, the sea uses its sharper picture.
- **Aztec:** the armour closet under the exhaust hatch is clear (no stone
  slab inside it, no metal box sticking into the room beside it), and the
  computer room's wall no longer blocks the vent.
- **Dam:** shots no longer stop in mid-air near the dam, and the fog follows
  the camera's height as in the release.
- **Shot-out lamps** darken the room around them.
- **Egyptian:** the pool's water is back. **Caverns:** its water is drawn
  without fog, as GoldenEye draws it.
- **Smaller fixes:** the sky colour fills in under the dome, Facility's
  hazard stripes and Control's stair stripes no longer show faces the
  release hides, and lettering painted on black (crates, Dam's CTON) has a clear
  background.

## Bean props, characters and guns

- **Shots** hit props and guards where their HD models are drawn, not where
  the N64 shapes were.
- **The monitors** show the release's own pictures (46 screens).
- **Characters:** hats sit on the HD heads, the seams between bodies and
  heads are welded, and lenses draw after the face.
- **Props:** shiny props without texture coordinates get their reflection
  (Silo's satellite, the cars' windows), a windowed door's glass goes when
  shot out, the military truck has its paired rear wheels again, Jungle's
  vines are clean at the edges, and distant props keep their full detail.
- **Colours from the Community Edition, now on retail copies too:** the
  crates and ammo crate, the runway gun, the metal chair, the tuning
  console, Complex's railings and the stool. Bunker II's cell doors, the
  wooden table's legs and Temple's crypt doors are recoloured, and
  Egyptian's crypt block repainted.
- **Mines** stick to the HD surface of a prop or door instead of sinking
  into it.
- **Guns:** the release's muzzle flashes, lettering that reads the right
  way on both sides, and the KF7's front sight on the barrel. Guards and
  other players hold the RPG-7 and the Moonraker by the grip (with
  Detailed Guns off).

## Guns in your hand

- **Labels and stencils no longer flicker** on the N64 guns: the rocket
  launcher's labels and its grip, in VR and on the flat screen.

## Multiplayer

- **Device:** the launcher's roster (was "Plays") and the in-game pause
  table show what everyone plays on: Quest, Quest (flat), PC VR, PC,
  Android, iPhone/iPad, Apple TV, Vision Pro or Steam Frame.
- **Who's playing:** your device type, app version and controls go to the
  lobby service as anonymous daily totals, shown on
  [lobbies.goldeneyevr.com](https://lobbies.goldeneyevr.com). See the
  updated [privacy policy](https://goldeneyevr.com/privacy).

## Speedrun and ghost races

- **Guards killed during a ghost race drop their guns and items again**
  (#263).
- **VR or flat** is now judged over the whole run, so VR runs no longer show
  as flat screen. A run that switches between the two shows as **Mixed**.
  The runs already on the boards are now labelled VR.

## Apple TV and flat screens

- **Apple TV:** online games no longer close the app when voice starts (the
  TV has no microphone), the remote moves one step per swipe, and the Apple
  TV 4K (1st gen) should no longer freeze at launch (#260, thanks to e-ee;
  not yet tried on a real one).
- **Every flat screen:** a game pad can reach the Friends panel, the
  Controls page has a **Pad vibration** setting, and Apple's full game pads
  are asked to vibrate.
- **Flickering decals** (Bunker II's bullet holes, Frigate's hull "06") are
  fixed on every platform, the Quest included.

## Help wanted and known issues

- **Bean throats:** a short-necked head on a guard with an open collar can
  leave a gap at the throat.
- **Bean grenades and mines:** please report one left in the hand after a
  throw.
- **Apple TV:** the Siri Remote and game pads on a real Apple TV.
- Otherwise as listed for [v0.5.20](v0.5.20.md#help-wanted-and-known-issues).

## Update and compatibility

**Quest:** use **Update** in the headset launcher, or sideload
`GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.21.apk`. **PC:** the PC launcher's updater. On
iPhone/iPad, install the new IPA with SideStore, or use TestFlight.

Android version: `0.5.21`; versionCode: `134`; network protocol: `27`.
v0.5.21 plays online only with v0.5.21: everyone in a game needs to update.

**Dedicated servers:** re-run your setup line to update.

- **Windows PC** (in PowerShell; sets up WSL for you if needed):
  `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.ps1 | iex`
- **Linux or a cloud server** (Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13, or newer):
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.sh | sudo bash`

No download contains game data: bring your own GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM, your
own patched ROM for a hack campaign, and for Bean, your own copy of the XBLA
release.
