# GoldenEye VR v0.5.2

The Dam speedboat now rides on the water and drives in VR, and two Bean wound
crashes are fixed.

## Dam speedboat

- The hull floats on the reservoir. It used to hang at the jetty's height,
  well above the water.
- The left (move) stick drives it, in VR and on the 2D screen: up goes ahead,
  left and right steer. In VR it used to not move at all.
- The boat starts clear of the north dock and stops at the jetty and the docks
  instead of sailing through them.
- The view no longer shakes while you drive.
- Getting on and off:
  - Grip at the boat's edge to board.
  - Grip near the island landing or back at the jetty to step off.
  - B does the same unless Grip use is on, or forced on by hand reload with
    GoldenEye X. There B keeps ejecting magazines.
- On the 2D screen the right stick turns you while you're aboard.

## Project Bean (XBLA visuals)

- Fixes two crashes in the bullet-wound drawing (reports 53f092b3 on Facility
  and 71b515e8 on Dam). Guards share head models, and a wound could read
  another guard's data. Wounds are also cleared when a guard's slot is reused
  and when a stage resets.

## Validation

- Checked on Quest 3 (Dam, XBLA visuals, GoldenEye X with hand reload):
  - boarding and stepping off by grip at the jetty;
  - driving and steering;
  - the hull's waterline;
  - the dock stopping the boat;
  - B left to ejecting magazines;
  - wounds on many guards at once with no crash.
- Native regressions cover:
  - the boat's height, the dock, boarding and stepping off, and the B and
    grip-use rules (under ASan/UBSan);
  - the wound crashes' two invalid addresses, shared heads, slot reuse and
    stage reset.

## Update and multiplayer compatibility

Download `GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.2.apk` or use **Update** in the headset launcher.
Updating over an earlier release keeps your settings. This APK contains no
GoldenEye, XBLA or Perfect Dark game data; use your existing game files.

Android version: `0.5.2`; versionCode: `82`; network protocol: `21`.
v0.4.16 through v0.5.2 share the same multiplayer protocol.
