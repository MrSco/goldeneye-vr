# GoldenEye VR v0.5.27

**Volumetric 3D explosions and lingering smoke** arrive as an optional graphics
mode, **animated monitors** scroll cleanly again on 64-bit builds, and the
**sniper/rifle scope on Steam Frame** is fixed. **Network protocol 27,
unchanged:** v0.5.27 plays online with v0.5.26, v0.5.25, v0.5.24, v0.5.23 and
v0.5.22.

## Volumetric explosions and smoke (#249)

- **Optional 3D volumetric fireballs and smoke clouds:** enable under
  **Graphics → Explosions** (`Classic` / `Volumetric`; **Classic** by default,
  live-toggleable without restarting).
- **True 3D, world-anchored volume:** analytical ray-marched spheres driven by
  a procedural 3D noise volume stay anchored in world space as you walk or look
  around them instead of rotating like flat camera-facing sprites, while
  matching classic smoke thickness and linger time.
- **Built for Quest 120 Hz chaos:** FP16 fragment shading, startup shader
  pre-warming (eliminating first-explosion hitches), and spatial cluster merging
  hold framerate even when multiple grenades detonate together (measured at
  120 Hz on Quest 3 with 2× MSAA and Bean).
- Works in VR (both eyes and magnified scopes) and on flat screens.

## Fixes

- **Steam Frame scope (#321):** fixed two bugs that made rifle scopes appear as
  a solid black circle shifted off to the right in the right eye on Steam Frame
  (Mesa/Zink under Lepton):
  - Scope targets now render into a 2-layer `GL_OVR_multiview` array framebuffer
    and blit layer 0 to the 2D lens texture when native multiview is active,
    preventing strict OpenGL ES drivers from rejecting `num_views = 2` draws
    with `GL_INVALID_OPERATION`.
  - The SteamVR HUD/scope projection layer now renders each eye separately via
    core `glFramebufferTextureLayer` instead of dynamically indexing a `mat4[]`
    by `gl_ViewID_OVR` (and the pointer/hub multiview shaders now select explicit
    per-eye uniforms), aligning the lens and in-game HUD quads in both eyes.
- **Scrolling monitors (#309):** sign-extend 32-bit negative scroll deltas in
  monitor animation scripts (`src/game/propobj.c`) and wrap pure-wrap UVs into
  `[0, 1)` so scrolling green text, bar graphs, waves and global maps (such as
  Dam's control room monitors) animate cleanly on all 64-bit builds instead of
  stretching into solid stripes.

## Update and compatibility

**Quest:** use **Update** in the headset launcher, or sideload
`GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.27.apk`. **PC:** the PC launcher's updater. On
iPhone/iPad, install the new IPA with SideStore, or use TestFlight
(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Vision Pro — all three TestFlight builds include
iCloud sync).

Android version: `0.5.27`; versionCode: `140`; network protocol: `27`.
v0.5.27 plays online with v0.5.26, v0.5.25, v0.5.24, v0.5.23 and v0.5.22.

**Dedicated servers:** re-run your setup line to update.

- **Windows PC** (in PowerShell; sets up WSL for you if needed):
  `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.ps1 | iex`
- **Linux or a cloud server** (Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13, or newer):
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.sh | sudo bash`

No download contains game data: bring your own GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM, your
own patched ROM for a hack campaign, and for Bean, your own copy of the XBLA
release.
