# GoldenEye VR v0.4.11

GE-X magazine weapons with independent VR fits, guard bullet tracer collision fix,
and outdoor room portal visibility fixes for Statue and Depot.

> **Bring your own game.** This APK contains no GoldenEye or Perfect Dark game
> data. You need a legally obtained ROM of the USA GoldenEye 007 cartridge you
> own. Installing over an earlier release keeps your ROM and settings.

## GE-X magazine weapons and VR fits — PR #137

- **GE-X magazine weapons.** Adds GE-X PP7 and silenced PP7, DD44, Klobb, ZMG,
  D5K and silenced D5K, Phantom, and AR33 alongside the existing KF7.
  Weapon definitions supply magazine bindings, reload timing, muzzle points,
  screen anchors, and texture mappings. GoldenEye retains firing rules, ammo, and sounds.
- **Physical reload grips & ammo conservation.** Physical reloads keep grip
  ownership until release, conserve partial magazines, and use adjustable
  insertion targets.
- **Per-weapon VR fits.** Gun fit provides support rotation, held-magazine preview,
  magazine-well adjustment, and independent installed-magazine mesh fitting.
  Defaults are baked in from headset calibrations (including PP7, DD44, Klobb,
  ZMG, D5K, Phantom, and AR33).
- **Installed magazine mesh control.** Component 6 adjusts visible magazine mesh
  placement independently of reload grab and insertion targets.

## Guard bullet tracers — PR #136

- **Tracer collision.** In solo mode, bullet tracers now test against Bond's player
  cylinder (`CDTYPE_PLAYERS`) instead of passing through and sparking on walls or
  hands behind the player. Rockets and grenades preserve original clearance behavior.

## Room pop-in fix (Statue & Depot) — PR #133

- **Portal visibility.** Neighboring rooms are drawn when their bounds are on screen
  even if the portal box test missed them, eliminating the black wedge at the start
  of Statue and missing fence chunks in Depot. Visible rooms load in the same frame
  instead of waiting on the retail three-room budget.

## Branding and launcher — PR #134

- Updated hero and square logo assets in README and launcher header.

## Upgrade notes

Android version: `0.4.11`; versionCode: `64`; network protocol: `18` (unchanged;
fully compatible with v0.4.6–v0.4.10).
