# v0.4.6

- **VR settings on the watch:** in a mission, the last row of the watch's
  Game Options page opens a VR page: turning, turn speed, vignette, watch
  face, aim steadying, motion throw, the grip gestures, hand reload and
  per-gun recoil, saved like the launcher's.
- **Smooth-turn speed:** a slider on the Comfort tab, 45 to 240 degrees per
  second (120 as before).
- **Grip gestures** (Controls > Gestures..., each its own switch; anywhere
  else the grip aims as before), from the upstream GEVR PC port:
  - hip holster (WIP): squeeze at your own hip to holster, again to draw;
  - grip use: a door, switch or console you reach, as B would use it;
  - grip to hand (WIP, off by default): a gun on the floor goes into that hand;
  - mine re-grab: your own stuck remote mine, or a proximity mine still
    arming, comes back.
  The watch gesture switch moved to this page.
- **Hand reload (WIP, off by default):** pull an SMG's or rifle's magazine
  with the off hand, or sweep a pistol, shotgun or dual-wielded gun across
  your chest. Auto-reload and B/Y reloads stop while it is on.
- **Per-gun recoil (off by default):** Perfect Dark VR's recoil profiles on
  GoldenEye's guns. The DD44 kicks at half the Cougar's strength and
  silenced guns kick less than their plain versions. A two-handed hold
  leaves almost no kick on any gun and tightens the bullet spread (not
  shotguns).
- **Game rules** (Play > Game rules..., both off by default): mines stick to
  guards; bodies stay (12, 24 or 48).
- **Launcher cheats:** 2x or 10x Health, 2x Armor, Max ammo, Extra weapons.
- **When hit** (Comfort tab, stereo): no knockback (on by default), keep
  firing when hit, and a switch for the red flash.
- **Statue and Cradle online**, from the cartridge's multiplayer setups.
- **Detailed guns in other hands:** guards and other players hold the
  first-person gun models, launchers with their rocket (`DetailedGuns=0` in
  goldeneye-vr.ini turns them off). A stage's HD gun textures are decoded at
  load, so the first switch to a gun shows them.
- **Floors:** remote players' bodies and Bond's floor recovery use Perfect
  Dark's floor finder under the whole body, not one point.
- **Decals:** room decals no longer draw through nearer geometry (the
  shadows under the Dam's start bridge showed on its beams). Decal walls
  over doorways stay whole (#72, #84), and Frigate's "06" and port windows
  still draw.
- **Melee** on the Dam and Surface reaches as far as on other stages.
- **The watch pause screen** shows on the virtual screen at its place in
  the room instead of following your head.
- **Fixes:** the level music returns after the death sting when someone
  else is dead too; ROMs that are not the USA cartridge are refused in
  every byte order, and the launcher stops on one (#108).

Android version name: `0.4.6`; version code: `59`; network protocol `18`.
A match may now be on Statue or Cradle, which v0.4.5 rejects, so v0.4.6
plays only with v0.4.6.

## Validation

The port's native and Python suites (multiplayer, launcher UI, pause UI,
surfaces, v0.4.3, VR display, watch grip) pass and the signed release APK
builds. Headset checks on one Quest 3: the watch VR page, grip use and the
holster, mines on guards, the magazine reload, per-gun recoil one- and
two-handed, the Dam bridge and Frigate's decals, melee on the Dam, the
detailed guns, and Cradle hosted alone.

Not yet checked on two headsets: Statue and Cradle matches, remote bodies at
ledges, other players' guns, and the game rules in co-op. Grip to hand,
hand reload and the hip holster are works in progress.
