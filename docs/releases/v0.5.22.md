# GoldenEye VR v0.5.22

Mostly for the Apple TV and the other flat screens. Bean (the XBLA look) now
draws on the graphics chip there too, so it runs far faster; the Siri Remote
works with button presses; a device that can't keep up plays a steady 30
instead of an uneven rate; and the Apple TV HD runs. Game pads vibrate on
every flat screen, Xbox and DualSense triggers buzz as you shoot, and a pad
works in the headset too. Vision Pro gets a fix for its ghosting and a
keyboard. And the flat screens get GoldenEye's own **split screen**, 2 to 4
players on one screen. **Network protocol 27, unchanged:** v0.5.22 plays
online with v0.5.21.

## Split screen (Windows, Linux, Apple TV, phones and tablets)

- **2 to 4 players on one screen,** GoldenEye's own split, offline. Choose
  **Multiplayer**, and everyone presses to join: A or Start on a pad, Enter
  on the keyboard, a tap on a phone. The order you join in is your player
  number; B or Escape leaves, Start or Enter plays once two have joined.
  Then the usual Multiplayer options, with Players set to who joined.
- **The keyboard and mouse are one player,** each pad another: a laptop and
  one pad are enough for two. The keyboard can be any player, and the mouse
  looks for whichever player it is.
- **Nobody is pushed to player 2 by a pad:** with no split screen, every pad
  plays as you, alongside the keyboard, so a pad plugged in mid-mission
  plays at once.
- **Each player's pad** rumbles and buzzes its triggers for that player
  alone. The HUD stays in each player's own view.
- **On the Apple TV** two pads are needed: the Siri Remote doesn't join.

## Faster Bean on flat screens

- **Bean on the graphics chip:** Bean's rooms, props and characters now draw
  on the GPU on Apple TV, iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets, as
  they already did in the headset. They were all walked on the CPU before.
  On an Apple TV HD, Dam with Bean went from 15 to 40 frames a second.
- **A steady half rate:** when a device can't keep up with the screen in a
  level (below 55 of 60 frames a second for 5 seconds), it plays that level
  at a steady 30 instead of an uneven rate, which judders on a TV. Bean and
  the N64 graphics are judged apart, so a device can play the N64 graphics
  at 60 and Bean at 30. The launcher's Bean page says when it happened.
  `Video.AutoHalfRate=0` in goldeneye.ini turns it off.

## Apple TV

- **The Apple TV HD runs.** It stopped at startup ("no ES 3 config").
- **The Siri Remote is buttons only** (#268, e-ee's suggestion). Its touch
  surface was too touchy and dragged the launcher's sliders.
  - In the launcher: click the ring's edge (a 1st generation remote: the
    touch surface's edge) to move, hold it to keep going, click the centre
    to choose, Back to go back.
  - In the game: the edges walk and strafe, the centre fires, Play/Pause
    changes gun (hold it to pause), Back opens doors.
- **No more crash** when an Apple TV goes to the home screen during an
  online match (#268, thanks to e-ee).
- **The file page** (your ROM, your XBLA copy, the Community Edition
  updater) shows a QR code: scan it with your phone's camera to send the
  file.
- **A new home-screen icon:** Frozen Inferno.

## Game pads

- **Pads vibrate on every flat screen** (Windows, Linux, Apple TV, iPhone,
  iPad, Android): a shot and a hit, as a Rumble Pak does. Changing Pad
  vibration in the settings gives a buzz at the new strength. Tested with a
  Switch Pro Controller and an 8BitDo SN30 Pro+ (#268, thanks to e-ee).
- **Trigger feedback** (e-ee's suggestion, #268): a shot buzzes the trigger
  that fired it, with an Xbox One or Series pad's trigger motors or a
  DualSense's adaptive triggers (Windows, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV). **Trigger feedback** under Controls,
  on by default; its strength follows Pad vibration.
- **A pad in the headset (Quest, PC VR):** it works as a pair of Touch
  controllers, in the launcher, the game and its panels, so the virtual
  screen plays like the flat game. In stereo, the gun still aims where a
  controller points.

## Vision Pro

- **Ghosting while walking or turning:** the picture is redrawn between game
  frames, as on the Quest, and a frame held over two refreshes moves with
  the scene (SpaceWarp in the Vision Pro runtime). Tried in the simulator.
- **The keyboard** comes up for text boxes and the debug log's note.

## Help wanted and known issues

- **Untried on real hardware:** Xbox and DualSense trigger feedback; a pad
  in the Quest and PC VR headsets; Vision Pro's ghosting fix; the half rate
  on Android phones and PCs; split screen on an Apple TV with two pads, on
  a phone, and with 3 or 4 players on a slower device. Reports welcome.
- **Grenade smoke** turning with the headset (#249) is still open.
- Otherwise as listed for [v0.5.21](v0.5.21.md#help-wanted-and-known-issues).

## Update and compatibility

**Quest:** use **Update** in the headset launcher, or sideload
`GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.22.apk`. **PC:** the PC launcher's updater. On
iPhone/iPad, install the new IPA with SideStore, or use TestFlight
(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Vision Pro).

Android version: `0.5.22`; versionCode: `135`; network protocol: `27`.
v0.5.22 plays online with v0.5.21.

**Dedicated servers:** re-run your setup line to update.

- **Windows PC** (in PowerShell; sets up WSL for you if needed):
  `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.ps1 | iex`
- **Linux or a cloud server** (Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13, or newer):
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.sh | sudo bash`

No download contains game data: bring your own GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM, your
own patched ROM for a hack campaign, and for Bean, your own copy of the XBLA
release.
