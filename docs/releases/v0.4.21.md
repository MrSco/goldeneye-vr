# GoldenEye VR v0.4.21

Tomorrow Never Dies 64 starts again: v0.4.20 crashed launching the
campaign, and then after the Rare logo. Co-op mission starts no longer
leave players stuck in scenery, and guards' muzzle flashes no longer show
through trees and walls.

This APK contains no GoldenEye, XBLA or Perfect Dark game data. Use your
existing USA GoldenEye 007 ROM. Updating over an earlier release keeps your
settings.

## Tomorrow Never Dies 64

- TND64 Expanded 06-22 rebuilt GoldenEye's animation data, moving every
  animation but the first. The game read them where GoldenEye has them and
  crashed as the campaign started. It now reads TND's own animation tables
  and finds each animation where TND put it, as Dab's Mod does.
- TND64 keeps GoldenEye's Rare logo but moved it. The title read it from
  GoldenEye's address and crashed after the logo spun. It now reads it where
  TND keeps it.
- Goldfinger 64 and GoldenEye load as before.

## Co-op mission starts (#164)

- On v0.4.20 the first player couldn't move at Jungle's start, and on
  Train, Control, Caverns and Cradle neither player could. Every player's
  start, the first player's included, is now checked against the level's
  loaded scenery at standing height. A player whose spot is blocked starts
  at the nearest clear spot beside it. A clear start pad is kept as before.
- Solo starts are unchanged.

## Guards' muzzle flashes

- Guards and other players hold the detailed first-person gun models
  (`DetailedGuns`, on by default), and their muzzle flash was drawn the way
  your own gun's is: on top of everything. A guard firing from behind a
  tree trunk or a wall showed its flash through it, in the headset and on
  the screen. Their flashes now hide behind the world; your own gun's flash
  is unchanged.

## Validation and remaining checks

- On real ROMs, all 173 of TND's moved animations read back as TND's own and
  its logo is found where its layout says; Goldfinger 64's campaign check
  still passes. TND64 loaded into its first mission in the headset.
- The rest of the TND campaign needs play, including its Girl Power
  options.
- Co-op starts: the placement test runs the game's own collision code and
  fails on v0.4.20; the floor audit passes for all twenty missions. Two
  headsets still need to start Jungle, Train, Control, Caverns and Cradle,
  then the rest and a late join. In Jungle, check that Natalya follows once
  the first player can move (reported stuck in co-op on v0.4.20); on this
  build she moved normally in Jungle in the headset.
- Muzzle flashes: a guard firing from behind a Jungle trunk is hidden by it
  in the headset. Each of the 13 cartridge guns with a flash is checked as
  the game loads it.

## Installation and multiplayer compatibility

Download `GoldenEye-VR-v0.4.21.apk` or use **Update** in the headset launcher.
Install it over your current version using your preferred installer.

Android version: `0.4.21`; versionCode: `77`; network protocol: `21` (unchanged).
v0.4.16 through v0.4.21 use the same multiplayer protocol.
