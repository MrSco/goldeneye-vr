# GoldenEye VR v0.4.23

Steadier aiming at 90 and 120 Hz, tidier body slots, each hand's ammo on its
own hand, and a fix for a Dam speedboat crash.

## Aiming and comfort

- Aim steadying now updates every headset frame, including in-between
  redraws, so the gun and scope no longer hold still between game frames.
  Its strength is matched to the 60 Hz feel at any display rate: fast hand
  moves follow as quickly at 90 and 120 Hz as at 60.
- Screen shake (explosions and the like) is off in VR. Walking head bob stays
  off; recoil and weapon animations are unchanged.

## Body slots

- The chest (knives, thrown) and belt (gadgets) now show the item a grip
  would take, at half size and centred on the slot, so they stay out of the
  way. The hip pistols and reload magazines are unchanged.
- The watch laser shows on the belt as the watch alone, face up, at the size
  it is on your wrist, instead of the whole two-arm rig.

## HUD

- Dual-wielding: each gun's ammo count now sits on its own hand. The off
  hand's count used to sit beside the gun hand's on the gun hand.

## Dam speedboat

- Fixes a crash after the speedboat was destroyed or removed (report
  252ac6ae). The boat is a normal prop now; if the hull is lost while you
  ride it, you land back on the north jetty instead of mid-reservoir.

## Project Bean

- Hidden and fully culled Bean meshes are skipped before any setup, and
  mirrored skins stay on the GPU. On Quest 3 with XBLA visuals on, fresh
  frames were 0.3-0.9 ms faster in moving and wall-contact phases; with only
  the Bean pack (XBLA visuals off) these paths don't run and nothing changes.
  120 Hz with XBLA rooms is still over budget when turning.

## Validation

- Checked on Quest 3: body-slot models and their slot centres, the watch on
  the belt, dual-wield ammo panels, and A/B frame timing against v0.4.22
  (three runs per build, Dam). Details:
  [VR tracking and Bean checks](https://github.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/blob/main/docs/vr-tracking-bean-validation.md),
  [crash 252ac6ae](https://github.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/blob/main/docs/crash-252ac6ae-dam-boat.md).
- Native regressions cover the steadying response across 60/90/120 Hz, the
  boat's lifecycle under ASan/UBSan, slot scale and centring, and the
  two-hand ammo split.
- Still worth play: destroying the boat mid-crossing, and the chest and belt
  models at your own slot positions.

## Update and multiplayer compatibility

Download `GoldenEye-VR-v0.4.23.apk` or use **Update** in the headset launcher.
Updating over an earlier release keeps your settings. This APK contains no
GoldenEye, XBLA or Perfect Dark game data; use your existing game files.

Android version: `0.4.23`; versionCode: `79`; network protocol: `21`.
v0.4.16 through v0.4.23 share the same multiplayer protocol.
