# GoldenEye VR v0.5.25

Hand reloads and the two-handed rifle bat now work on **GoldenEye's own
original gun models**, **flat-screen play runs at high refresh rates** (120 /
144 / 180 Hz+ with smooth mouse look and 3D locomotion), **PC gets full
keyboard/mouse remapping and cleaner launcher & in-game settings**, and two
high-priority crashes (Steam Frame / Lepton launch crash `#276` and a ROM-hack
blood-stain hang) are fixed. **Network protocol 27, unchanged:** v0.5.25 plays
online with v0.5.24, v0.5.23 and v0.5.22.

## VR: original guns' hand reloads, rifle bat & polish

- **Hand reload on GoldenEye's original models:** GoldenEye's own guns now have
  removable magazines, including the Cougar, grenade/rocket launchers (with
  GoldenEye's actual `PchrrocketZ` rocket model), and the Golden Gun.
- **Two-handed rifle bat:** Bean's sniper rifle now works as a two-handed bat
  held in the pistol-grip glove, and GoldenEye's own rifle uses the default
  fitted bat grip with the stock seated in the off hand's palm.
- **GoldenEye X shotguns** now take their shells directly from the gun during
  hand reload, and the GoldenEye X weapon switch is blocked cleanly in hack
  campaigns.
- **Bean visual polish:** bullet holes keep their colour, and the watch readout
  no longer flickers on GoldenEye's dial.
- **PC VR startup fix:** OpenXR initialization no longer fails when a runtime
  refuses an eye image before it is acquired.

## Flat screens: high refresh rates, smooth mouse & locomotion (#274)

- **Frame rate options** (**Display → Frame rate**): `Auto` (matches your
  monitor's refresh rate, stepping down automatically only if needed), fixed
  caps (`30`, `60`, `90`, `120`, `144`, `165`, `180`, `240`), or `Uncapped`.
  Half rate uses the nearest whole number of refreshes on displays 30 doesn't
  divide (such as 144 Hz).
- **High-refresh mouse look & 3D locomotion:** in-between refreshes redraw the
  world with live mouse rotation and interpolated confirmed 3D camera
  translation — eliminating 60 Hz mouse ghosting and circle-strafing jitter —
  and stay active while aiming.
- **Flat PC rendering fixes:** enabled desktop static meshes, fixed
  vertex-ring overflow glitches, and replayed `G_CLEAR_DEPTH_EXT` depth clears
  during redraws so weapons no longer ghost or clip when looking down.

## Launcher, controls & PC quality of life

- **PC keyboard, mouse & gamepad settings:** full keybind remapping (including
  a dedicated **Previous weapon** bind), mouse sensitivity, and gamepad options
  in both the launcher and in-game settings.
- **Cleaner launcher & in-game tabs:** launcher tabs are regrouped behind a
  single context visibility table (`Play > General` instead of a duplicate
  `Play` sub-tab), in-game `Controls` nests `Keyboard & mouse`, `Gamepad`, and
  `Look` as sub-tabs, and `Mods/Fun` fits cleanly without truncation.
- **PC display & window polish:** added a **Fullscreen** toggle in Display
  settings, live FPS / frame-time / resolution stats in the Windows title bar
  while windowed, and a **Quit** button with confirmation modals (also used for
  returning to the launcher).

## Crash & hang fixes

- **Steam Frame / Lepton launch crash (#276):** guarded a null
  `ClipboardManager` in `SDLClipboardHandler` so the app no longer throws a
  `NullPointerException` on startup under Lepton.
- **ROM-hack blood-stain hang (reports #41):** bounded the `PointUsage`
  linked-list walk and validated collision records in `chrCreateBloodStain()`
  so custom/ROM-hack models with cyclic or zero-filled tables can no longer
  hang the game thread.

## Update and compatibility

**Quest:** use **Update** in the headset launcher, or sideload
`GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.25.apk`. **PC:** the PC launcher's updater. On
iPhone/iPad, install the new IPA with SideStore, or use TestFlight
(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Vision Pro).

Android version: `0.5.25`; versionCode: `138`; network protocol: `27`.
v0.5.25 plays online with v0.5.24, v0.5.23 and v0.5.22.

**Dedicated servers:** re-run your setup line to update.

- **Windows PC** (in PowerShell; sets up WSL for you if needed):
  `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.ps1 | iex`
- **Linux or a cloud server** (Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13, or newer):
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.sh | sudo bash`

No download contains game data: bring your own GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM, your
own patched ROM for a hack campaign, and for Bean, your own copy of the XBLA
release.
