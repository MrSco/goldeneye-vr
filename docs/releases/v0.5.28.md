# GoldenEye VR v0.5.28

**Online matches are set up in the game now**, on the original N64 screens,
and a deathmatch party gathers in a **pre-match lobby** before each match.
**Split screen goes online** (up to four players on one screen join any online
mode), **couch co-op** plays the campaign together on one screen, and a new
**cross-platform sync** keeps settings, saves, ROMs and packs the same on every
device through your own Google Drive. **Network protocol 29:** v0.5.28 plays
online only with v0.5.28, so update every device and dedicated server.

## Online match setup in the game (PR #343)

- **The original screens, in the match:** Select Stage's photo grid for the
  host's map and everyone's next-map vote (each photo shows its votes), the
  Select Character portraits, Handicap and Teams. Each draws in its player's
  own view, split screen included, and on a head-locked panel in VR.
- **The Multiplayer Options folder:** the match's rules in the original menu's
  look (players, scenario, level, length, weapons, characters, health,
  handicaps, dual wield, loadouts, bots, next round; Horde: level, starting
  wave, friendly fire, AI ally). Host-only rows are dimmed for everyone else.
- **Handicaps:** a host rule (off by default) gives each player their own
  health; the host can set anyone's, bots included.
- **Horde setup break:** every run starts in a break with no guards and no
  damage, and waits for the host's START WAVE.
- **A slimmer launcher:** one **Host** button goes straight into the match;
  character and loadout are chosen in the game, which remembers them.
- **The game's screens take the laser and the mouse**, in the lobby and in a
  match.

## Pre-match lobby

- A deathmatch party waits on the title stage before each match: pick your
  folder, press **READY**, vote, and the host's **START** loads the map. After
  each match everyone comes back to it.
- **Warmup** (a host rule, Off by default, or 30 s / 60 s / 2 min) is the old
  practice round after START. Turning the **Lobby** rule off brings back the
  old drop-in warmup.
- One player on a screen (flat, or any headset) gets the full pause window for
  the lobby; split screen gets the folder.
- Dedicated servers run the lobby too: the match starts when everyone is READY,
  or 60 s after the first player is.
- The internet game list shows **(in lobby)** for a game still gathering.

## Split screen online and on the couch (PR #332)

- **Online split screen:** 1 to 4 players on one screen host or join online
  Deathmatch, Co-op and Horde. Each controller joins by pressing START during
  the match, and the screen splits for it; a new player picks a character on
  the portrait screen first.
- **Couch co-op** (flat builds): the campaign together on one screen. Co-op now
  sits under Multiplayer (Versus or Co-op first).
- **Couch drop-in:** in an offline split-screen match, a pad drops in mid-match
  with START.
- **No Teleport** host rule for online matches.

## Cross-platform sync (Google Drive)

- **Play → Sync:** sign in with Google and your settings, saves, ROMs and packs
  follow you between Quest, PC, Linux, Steam Frame and Apple devices. Only the
  game's own folder on your Drive is used.
- **Saves are never merged:** the save written last wins, and the replaced one
  stays on the device as a backup (the last five).
- **A save check at START:** if another device has a newer save, the game asks
  before you play over it.
- Which settings travel is one table shared with iCloud: a device's display,
  audio output and couch seats stay on that device.

## Vision Pro

- **Much smoother:** the game drew both eyes in turn for every draw, which cost
  Vision Pro a render pass per draw. Each eye is now drawn whole (simulator:
  8-10 fps to a steady 60 on Dam). SpaceWarp is off by default; it isn't needed
  now.

## Other changes

- **DualSense adaptive triggers (PR #333):** a gun fires once you pull past its
  trigger's bump; knives, throwables, fists, beam weapons and gadgets each get
  their own feel. **Adaptive triggers** turns it off.
- **Audio:** music volume now applies on the title, the intro and the menus;
  the N64 mix plays 6 dB quieter on every platform (iPhone, iPad and Apple TV
  were much louder than other apps), with a **Master volume** setting on top.
- **iCloud** keeps what a device already has when a TestFlight build is
  installed over a development one (no more downloading Bean again).
- **Clear ROM errors (PR #336):** a missing or wrong ROM says so and is no
  longer recorded as a crash; stale crash reports retire after a clean exit.
- **Flat builds (#337, PR #338):** Dam's distant mountain no longer draws over
  the sky by the first gatehouse (Android, iOS, Apple TV, flat PC).
- **VR players as others see them:** after a stick turn, other players saw a
  VR player's head turned to one side while they looked straight ahead. The
  stick now turns the body, and the headset only the head.

## Fixes from online play (PR #350)

- **Invites (#347):** an "Invited" mark lasts a minute and clears when the
  friend arrives, so a friend who dropped out can be invited again.
- **Slappers:** flat players were knocked through walls by a punch; the push is
  the game's size again.
- **Linux crash with mines and knives (likely #349):** a throw through a
  doorway overran a room buffer. Fixed on every platform, and the Linux build
  now writes crash reports (its handler was never compiled in).
- **Co-op over a VPN:** a co-op mission dropped the other player a few seconds
  after loading when the two devices talked over a 1280-byte path (Tailscale,
  WireGuard). The game's packets now fit it.
- **A player who vanishes** (a crash, a lost connection) now leaves the match
  and can rejoin; before, they stayed in the lobby.

## Update and compatibility

**Quest:** use **Update** in the headset launcher, or sideload
`GoldenEye-VR-v0.5.28.apk`. **PC:** the PC launcher's updater. On
iPhone/iPad, install the new IPA with SideStore, or use TestFlight
(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Vision Pro; the TestFlight builds include iCloud
sync).

Android version: `0.5.28`; versionCode: `141`; network protocol: `29`.
v0.5.28 plays online only with v0.5.28: older versions can't join it or host
for it.

**Dedicated servers:** re-run your setup line to update (needed: protocol 29).

- **Windows PC** (in PowerShell; sets up WSL for you if needed):
  `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.ps1 | iex`
- **Linux or a cloud server** (Ubuntu 24.04 or Debian 13, or newer):
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MrSco/goldeneye-vr/main/tools/gevr-server-setup.sh | sudo bash`

No download contains game data: bring your own GoldenEye 007 (USA) ROM, your
own patched ROM for a hack campaign, and for Bean, your own copy of the XBLA
release.
